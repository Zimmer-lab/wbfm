# Baseline vs. oscillation control: statistics summary

Generated by `fig_supp_baseline.ipynb` / `.py`. Full tables: `boxplot_stats.csv` (panel b), `stats_summary.csv` (Q1-Q3,
amplitudes, gate), `boxplot_stats_summed_brightness.csv` (same tests on the summed paper trace, for comparison).

**Baseline = mean green intensity per voxel above background** (paper green trace, which is the background-subtracted
SUM over the neuron's voxels, divided by the segmented volume per frame), median over the frames of each state.
States from the behavior annotation in data.h5 (turns and pauses excluded); >= 100 frames per state per neuron x dataset
(10 of 1576 pairs excluded). Groups as in Fig. 5b: gated n=9, ungated n=10, no encoding n=42; 36 datasets.

Two levels of analysis, BH-FDR across the 7 comparisons within each level:
- Neuron level: median across datasets; Wilcoxon signed-rank (paired) / Mann-Whitney U.
- Dataset level (neuron x dataset medians): linear mixed models. Paired: neuron fixed, random intercepts for dataset and
  neuron x dataset. Between groups: crossed random intercepts for dataset and neuron.
